Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TradeJournal ("the app") is a trading journal for logging and reviewing your own trades. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What data the app stores

Everything you enter — trades, notes, settings — is stored locally on your own device, using your browser's or device's local storage (IndexedDB). None of it is transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. TradeJournal has no server, no account system, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect, see, or have access to your trade data, personal information, or usage activity.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to any third party, because your data never leaves your device in the first place.

Backups and exports

The app lets you export your own data as a CSV or JSON file for backup purposes. These files are saved directly to your device by your browser or operating system — we have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can erase all stored data at any time from within the app (Settings → Erase all trades), or by clearing the app's storage/data through your device or browser settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's store page.
